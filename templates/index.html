Notes

Positions are J2000 catalog coordinates precessed to the session date. Refraction is ignored; it lifts objects about 0.05° at 20° altitude. Moon and Sun positions are accurate to about a quarter of a degree and a minute of time.

Catalog data: OpenNGC by Mattia Verga, licensed CC-BY-SA 4.0. Chart stars and constellation figures: d3-celestial by Olaf Frohn, BSD 3-Clause license.

Catalog magnitude is total brightness. A big nebula or galaxy spreads that light out, so it looks fainter per pixel than a compact cluster of the same magnitude.
